Who We Are

HerCodex is a collaborative team of BCA students from Khalsa College for Women who came together to explore technology beyond the classroom.

We are beginners with big curiosity, learning by building projects and participating in hackathons.

Our Mission

Our mission is to learn by building real projects, collaborate as a team, and grow as future technology creators.
